How to Play

A color word appears on screen written in a different color. Your goal is to tap the circle that matches the word, not the color it's written in. The game gets progressively harder with more colors and less time.

Frequently Asked Questions

The game is too fast!

The timer gets shorter as you level up. Focus on the word, not the color. Practice makes perfect!

How do I reset my progress?

Go to Settings (gear icon on the home screen) and tap "Reset Progress".

Ads

Colors Combo Reloaded is free to play and supported by ads. Ads appear occasionally after game over. You can also watch an optional ad to continue playing after a game over.
