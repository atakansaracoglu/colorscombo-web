Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Colors Combo Reloaded ("we", "our", or "the App") is developed by Atakan Saracoglu. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

The App itself does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. Game progress (high score, settings) is stored locally on your device only.

Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services that may collect information:

Google AdMob

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and use data to serve personalized or non-personalized ads. This may include:

  • Device identifiers (IDFA, when permitted)
  • IP address
  • Device and app information
  • Interaction data with ads

For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy.

App Tracking Transparency

On iOS 14.5 and later, the App will request your permission before tracking your activity across other companies' apps and websites. You can change this permission at any time in your device's Settings.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the third-party services, please contact us.

Data Retention

All game data is stored locally on your device. We do not maintain any servers or databases that store your data. Uninstalling the App removes all locally stored data.

Your Rights

Since the App does not collect personal data directly, there is no personal data for us to access, modify, or delete. For data collected by third-party ad services, please refer to their respective privacy policies.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
